On the evening of Monday, 19th October 2026, Anna and Mario Cappello, Gloria, John Paul, Fr. Jerome, Fr. Shawn and Jacek visited the Parish of St. Francis of Assisi in Łódź, Poland, for a special evening of missionary outreach.

The evening was an opportunity to share the joy of the Gospel and invite those present to deepen their personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

Mario offered an inspiring teaching on *Missionary Discipleship*, reminding everyone of the call to live as disciples of Christ and to share His love with others.

Anna then shared the story of the ICPE Mission and introduced her book, *A Cast of Light* (Polish title: [*Promyk Światła. Historia ICPE Mission*](/promyk-swiatla/)), which beautifully captures the Mission's 40-year journey of faith, missionary adventures and God's faithfulness through the years.

Following these sharings, Jacek invited those present to come forward and receive personal prayer from two prayer teams, providing an opportunity for a deeper encounter with the Lord.

The evening concluded on a joyful and grace-filled note. Many participants responded to the invitation to continue their journey of faith by signing up for the upcoming *Life in the Spirit Seminar*, organised by ICPE Mission Poland. Many also purchased copies of *A Cast of Light*, taking home a testimony of God's work through the ICPE Mission over the past four decades.

We give thanks to God for this beautiful evening of encounter, prayer and fellowship, and we pray that the seeds planted in the hearts of those present will continue to grow and bear fruit in their lives.
